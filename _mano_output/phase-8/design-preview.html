3D Motion

The same Anima.on() convenience layer, driving a 3D Icosahedron Card instead of a flat one.

Anima.on(card).position(Vector3(0.6, 0.0, 0.0), 0.4)

Same shared ExampleHeader, stage frame, and playback controls as every 2D playground — only the card changes. The Icosahedron (examples/playground/models/card.obj) is approximated here as a faceted gem: translucent violet facets, a bright rim where edges catch the light, and an emissive core glow — v2_stuff/icosahedron.png's look and feel, recoloured to the app's own accent / accent-soft palette instead of green, and with no text on any face. In Godot this comes from a ShaderMaterial (fresnel rim + emissive core), not a flat texture.
