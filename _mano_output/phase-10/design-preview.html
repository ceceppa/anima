Both buttons get the same treatment — neither is styled as more "primary" than the other. Fill: flat accent, a StyleBoxFlat so it can carry a border. Border: 2px accent-soft. Corner radius: 48px (half the 96px button, keeping it a true circle). No glow — an earlier pass tried one behind each button and dropped it for reading as visual noise rather than depth; the flat fill and border carry the definition on their own now.
